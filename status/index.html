DOJ has announced multiple HSS charging waves and guilty pleas, including additional cases in the broader 2026 Minnesota health-care-fraud takedown. 05 · How to read status

The verb matters more than the headline.

When a new story appears, locate the procedural verb first. It tells you what has actually changed.

Charged

A prosecutor has filed an accusation. Guilt has not been established.

Pleaded guilty

The defendant admitted guilt in court. A sentence usually comes later.

Convicted at trial

A judge or jury found the defendant guilty after trial. Sentencing remains a separate step.

Sentenced

A court imposed punishment after conviction. Prison time is only one possible component; restitution, forfeiture and supervision can also matter.

Pending

The available official record still shows unresolved charges or future proceedings. Pending does not mean guilty.

Separate case

One person can have more than one prosecution. Abdiaziz Farah's fraud sentence and juror-bribery sentence are different criminal cases and should remain distinct.

06 · Status method

A current page should be willing to expire.

Status changes. The right response is not to hide that limitation; it is to date every aggregate, link the release that established it, and update when a newer official source supersedes it.

Publication rule

MN Peace does not infer a current outcome from a charging document. A charge-era case brief remains a brief of that filing. This status desk supplies later procedural context from official releases and should yield to the court docket if the two ever diverge.

Want the original filing?

Current status tells you what happened later. The Record shows what prosecutors originally alleged, the counts they filed, the defendants named and the primary PDF where available.
