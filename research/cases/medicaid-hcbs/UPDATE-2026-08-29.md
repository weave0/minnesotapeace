## 5. New Medical Assistance Fraud offense — controlling sources acquired

### Statute

2026 Minn. Laws ch. 127, art. 7 creates **Minn. Stat. § 609.467**.  
Source record: `research/sources/src-mn-laws-2026-ch127-art7-medical-assistance-fraud.json`

Penalty ceilings in the enacted text:

- loss ≤ $100,000: up to 10 years / $20,000 fine;
- loss > $100,000 through $1M: up to 20 years / $100,000 fine;
- loss > $1M: up to 30 years / $1M fine.

Effective **Aug. 1, 2026** for crimes committed on or after that date.

### Sentencing Guidelines

Source record: `research/sources/src-mn-msgc-2026-medical-assistance-fraud-rankings.json`

2026 offense rankings:

- ≤ $100k → Severity Level 2;
- $100,001–$1M → Severity Level 6;
- > $1M → Severity Level 8.

Statutory maximums and guideline severity are separate concepts. Do not apply the new offense or 2026 rankings to pre-Aug. 1 conduct without conduct-date/legal analysis.
