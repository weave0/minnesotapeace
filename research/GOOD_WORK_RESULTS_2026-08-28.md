## Purpose

This pass follows the 2026-08-27 good-work map ledger. It does two things without lowering the evidence bar:

1. Re-check grant-only police/community projects against local primary sources and promote a label only when local evidence supports a stronger description.
2. Add Greater Minnesota civic-service examples outside policing, with concrete current or recent outputs.

Political affiliation is not inferred for public agencies, food shelves, donors, volunteers, beneficiaries, cities or counties. Faith identity is disclosed only where the organization itself states it.

## Map status upgrades

### Keewatin — Spartan Care Closet
Primary source:
- https://www.isd319.org/article/2533866

Nashwauk-Keewatin Schools reported on November 6, 2025 that students had access to essential items and snacks through the Spartan Care Closet. The district credited Keewatin Police Chief Chris Whitney with helping secure:
- $2,000 — Minnesota Chiefs of Police Foundation
- $1,000 — Greenway Lions Club
- $750 — Nashwauk Fire Relief
- $500 — Keewatin Legion
- $250 — Amvets
- $225 — Hibbing Walmart
- $125 — local residents

Total documented grants/donations listed by the school: **$4,850**.

Public status: **Reported student support · 2025–26 school year**.

This is stronger than the prior MCPF-only grant label because the school itself says students have access to the closet and identifies the support received.

## Greater Minnesota — non-police civic service

### Bemidji Community Food Shelf
Primary source:
- https://www.bcfsmn.org/

Current site reports 2025 impact:
- **13,063 visits**
- **41,576 individuals served**
- **891,576 pounds of food distributed**
- **736 donors**

The site also says more than 500 volunteers contribute and describes a 3.5-acre farm growing fresh produce for food-shelf distribution. The organization identifies itself as faith-based and inclusive and says its mission serves people experiencing food insecurity in Beltrami County and the Bemidji School District.

Public treatment: disclose the self-described faith basis; do not infer a political identity.

### ECHO Food Shelf — Mankato
Primary sources:
- https://www.echofoodshelf.org/
- https://www.echofoodshelf.org/need-food/

ECHO's current site says it distributes about **160,000 pounds of food per month** to approximately **1,700 households**. Current services include client-choice groceries, ready-to-eat food for people without storage/cooking facilities, summer supplemental food for children and Thanksgiving baskets.

Public treatment: concrete current service, no political or faith label inferred.

### Olmsted County + Channel One — November 2025 food distribution
Primary county source:
- https://www.olmstedcounty.gov/government/county-news-events/county-news/partnering-provide-food-those-need

Olmsted County reported that a **$200,000** county contribution helped Channel One Food Bank bridge disrupted food assistance during the November 2025 federal shutdown. On November 7, 2025, the county reported final figures of:
- **235,000 pounds** of dry goods and fresh produce distributed
- **3,900 neighbors** served
- **361 volunteers** participating

Each household received three boxes containing fresh foods, shelf-stable items and proteins.

Public treatment: county/nonprofit partnership; no partisan inference.

### Catholic Charities of the Diocese of St. Cloud
Primary sources:
- https://www.ccstcloud.org/annual-report
- https://www.ccstcloud.org/pack-the-porches-2026

The organization's 2025 annual report says its Food Shelf served more than **20,000 individuals** and distributed more than **2.6 million pounds of food**. The broader organization reports 2,642,714 pounds of food provided in 2025.

Its completed March 27, 2026 Pack the Porches drive reports:
- **6,615 pounds** of nonperishable food collected
- **$24,665** raised
- Coborn's matching food donations up to **$15,000**

Public treatment: disclose Catholic identity because the organization makes it explicit; feature the service outputs, not institutional praise.
