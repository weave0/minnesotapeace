# Civic-good sourcing ledger — 2026-08-27

Private research support for the public `/good/` page. This file stays in git and is excluded from the public Cloudflare artifact.

## Editorial scope

`Minnesotans Helping Minnesotans` is intentionally curated. It is not a census of Minnesota civic life and must not be described as politically representative.

Eligible lanes:
- Republican-affiliated organizations when affiliation is explicit and service is documented.
- Conservative organizations when the organization self-identifies or an authoritative source supports the label.
- Faith-based service organizations when faith is disclosed and service is documented.
- Law-enforcement and first-responder organizations with concrete community or family support.
- Nonpartisan civic/service organizations when useful to the story.

Excluded from this series by editorial scope:
- DFL party units.
- Democratic campaign committees.
- Organizations principally organized to elect Democratic candidates.

Important: exclusion is an editorial scope choice, not evidence that Democratic Minnesotans do not perform civic service. Do not infer the politics of employees, volunteers, donors, clients or beneficiaries from the organization serving them.

## Publication standard

Prefer acts over praise. Every public card should have at least one primary or authoritative source showing a concrete activity, measurable output, recurring program, grant, scholarship or form of direct aid. Political and faith labels travel with the story when material. Material controversies or corrections must not be omitted merely to preserve a positive frame.

## Public-purpose bridge

### Child and Adult Care Food Program
Source: https://www.fns.usda.gov/cacfp/why-cacfp-important
USDA describes CACFP as improving day-care quality and affordability for many low-income families and providing nutritious meals/snacks to millions of children, as well as qualifying adults in care.

### Summer Food Service Program
Source: https://www.fns.usda.gov/sfsp/faqs
USDA describes SFSP as providing nutritious meals during school vacation periods; open sites generally operate in low-income areas and offer free meals to children.

### Minnesota Housing Stabilization Services
Source: https://mn.gov/dhs/housing-income/housing-services/programs-and-services/housing-stabilization-services/
DHS describes the former Medicaid benefit as helping seniors and people with disabilities find and keep housing, increase housing stability, and avoid homelessness or institutionalization. Program ended Oct. 31, 2025.

## Protectors

### Minnesota Chiefs of Police Foundation — Community Partnership Grants
Sources:
- https://www.mnchiefs.org/community-grants
- https://www.mnchiefs.org/grant-resources
MCPF says the program exists to strengthen police-community relationships, trust, transparency, cooperation and partnerships. 2025 funding was about $100,000 to more than 50 departments. Future profiles should pull grant-recipient project pages individually rather than rely on generic descriptions.

### Minnesota 100 Club
Sources:
- https://mn100club.org/
- https://mn100club.org/roll-call/
Provides financial aid to families of Minnesota peace officers, firefighters, EMS/EMTs and corrections officers killed or seriously injured in the line of duty. Roll Call page reports more than $1M in benefits since 1972, nearly 30 awards in the prior two years, and $320,000 listed for 2026 when checked Aug. 27, 2026.

### Backing the Blue Line
Sources:
- https://www.backingtheblueline.org/
- https://www.backingtheblueline.org/family-support
- 2025 Blue Line Ball PDF indexed by search: https://www.backingtheblueline.org/_files/ugd/8ef51b_3277c06b343d4d8a87d3f01a91beeecb.pdf
Minnesota-founded 501(c)(3) supporting law-enforcement officers/families. 2025 materials reported 1,000+ Family Support submissions, gifts to 40+ officers injured in line of duty and support for 100+ officers involved in critical incidents. About page says support does not discriminate by political affiliation, religion, gender, race or sexual orientation.

### Minnesota Law Enforcement Explorer Association
Sources:
- https://www.mnleexplorer.org/
- https://www.mnleexplorer.org/education-scholarships
Program for ages roughly 14–20 sponsored by local law-enforcement agencies; emphasizes career exploration, life skills, service learning, character education and leadership. Scholarships available.

### Minnesota Fraternal Order of Police Foundation
Sources:
- https://www.mnfopfoundation.org/
- https://www.mnfopfoundation.org/scholarships
Supports officer families/community engagement and the MLEEA youth scholarship program.

## Neighbors

### Together for Good Minnesota
Sources:
- https://www.tfgood.org/minnesota
- https://www.tfgood.org/
Christian 501(c)(3) mobilizing churches/volunteers to support vulnerable children and parents through host families, parent advocates and practical support. Minnesota site says Twin Cities/Rochester operation has served families since 2017.

### Union Gospel Mission Twin Cities
Source: https://www.ugmtc.org/who-we-are/quick-facts/
Christian homelessness/hunger services. 2024 impact page reports 258,000+ meals, 115,246 shelter nights, 6,012 volunteers, 6,531 on-site mental-health appointments, plus education/support-service metrics.

### New Life Family Services
Sources:
- https://nlfs.org/about/
- https://nlfs.org/about/what-we-do/
- https://nlfs.org/services/
Explicit Christian/pro-life mission. Documents no-cost pregnancy testing/counseling, limited ultrasounds, STI services, parenting support/practical goods, licensed adoption and interim foster care. Always disclose ideological/faith context rather than describing as generic nonpartisan family services.

## Republican civic work

### Minnesota Young Republicans
Sources:
- https://www.minnesotayrs.org/
- https://mngop.com/affiliate-groups/
Own site explicitly describes conservative social engagement and electoral organizing. Its `Giving Back to the Community` section says it runs an annual food drive and other volunteer events. MNGOP lists Minnesota Young Republicans as a state-party affiliate.

### NW Metro Republican Women
Source: https://nwmetrogopwomen.org/
Own site says members focus on education, Republican grassroots political involvement and community service benefiting veterans. It says the organization is chartered by the Minnesota Federation of Republican Women. Published activity includes recurring support for veteran residents at Haven for Heroes.

### Republican Party of Minnesota affiliate roster
Source: https://mngop.com/affiliate-groups/
Useful as authoritative affiliation verification. Current listed groups include Minnesota Federation of Republican Women, Republican Seniors of Minnesota, Minnesota Young Republicans, Minnesota College Republicans, Hispanic Republican Assembly of MN, Log Cabin Republicans of Minnesota, Asian American Republicans of Minnesota, Minnesota Republican Liberty Caucus, Minnesota Organization of Republican Veterans and American Indian Republican Affiliate.

## Around Minnesota place stories — added 2026-08-28

Public `/good/#around` is a place-story layer, not a GIS map. Grouping is north / central / south. Western Minnesota is absent from this set; do not invent western stories to fill the hole.

Editorial method for these cards:
- Form: place → people → problem → action → result → source.
- City name is the kicker. Counts that come from a city, department or organization stay attributed.
- No organization logos.
- No MCPF-as-only-visible-source when a city, PD, school or church page exists.
- For Fairmont autism kits and Mankato Operation Turkey Drop, the MCPF Oct. 31, 2025 C-Notes list is the finding; journalism is a second “also reported” link.
- Do not treat MCPF grant-resources boilerplate as a finding.
- People on these cards are not labeled Republican or Democrat. Christ the King is labeled Lutheran because the church says so. ECHO has no faith/political affiliation label.
- Do not merge Belle Plaine Hook, Line and Heroes with Scott County’s Cedar Lake derby.
- Do not add Detroit Lakes, Adrian, Winthrop or other MCPF-list towns unless independently sourced as their own stories.

Excluded-from-this-set reminders: Detroit Lakes Shop with a Cop; Adrian Lights & Sirens of Giving; Winthrop Slide & Slice with Cops; Cannon Falls / Zumbrota / Goodhue County Sheriff skate items from the 2024 MCPF list; NC veterans nonprofit Hook Line & Heroes.

### bemidji-casting-with-cops — Bemidji, Beltrami, north
Primary: https://www.ci.bemidji.mn.us/uploads/dm/73247/State_of_the_City_2024

Contrary / context in the same address (do not moralize): PD operated four officers short in 2024; the address also makes a city crime-rate claim. Public card carries one quiet staffing clause only.
Also listed: MCPF 2025 C-Notes, “Bemidji Police Department – Casting with Cops.” Not used as the visible source because the city document exists.
Research-brief named additional partners (fire, DNR, HS fishing team). Public card follows the city document (PD, Parks, sheriff, local donors/sponsors).
