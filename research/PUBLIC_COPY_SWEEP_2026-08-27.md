# Public-copy sweep — 2026-08-27

This note tracks the cleanup requested after publication review.

## Remove from public voice

Avoid phrasing that describes the intended audience or tells the reader how to process our editorial machinery, including:

- “global reader”
- “for readers anywhere”
- “why outsiders should care”
- other drafting-language that explains the editorial objective instead of stating the underlying fact directly

## Replacement standard

Public copy should:

- state the legal or institutional distinction directly
- explain why a distinction matters in the case record
- use plain English without talking about “the reader”
- reserve process/methodology language for explicit methodology/source sections

This is a publication-language rule, not a factual-content change.
