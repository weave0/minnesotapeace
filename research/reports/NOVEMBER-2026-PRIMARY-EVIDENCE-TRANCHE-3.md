## Substantive findings, all classified CIVIL ALLEGED

**A. Explicit property status at filing (PDF p.2, ¶3):** The United States states the defendant parcels **had not been seized** and that the government was not seeking authority to seize them *at that time*. It planned notice, service and a notice of lis pendens. Later (PDF pp.17–18, ¶62 and prayer), it requests a warrant and eventual forfeiture. These are time/procedure-dependent statements, not evidence of a completed seizure or court disposition. Avoid newspaper-style "government seized the block."

**B. Distinct sponsor (PDF pp.6–8, ¶¶18–25):** The complaint specifically traces alleged child-nutrition reimbursement activity under **Partners in Nutrition (PIN)** and the **Addis Foundation Community Services**. PIN is **not** the organization Feeding Our Future. The October 30 DOJ public release used "Feeding Our Future fraud scheme" to describe the three-property civil action; this primary filing is more specific. MN Peace should link the factual transaction to **PIN's sponsorship as alleged** rather than merging separate sponsor networks. The complaint's language about PIN's alleged conduct is prosecutor allegation, not a conviction.

**C. Claimed meals vs alleged reimbursements (PDF pp.9–10, ¶¶32–34):** Prosecutors allege related entities submitted reimbursement claims for more than **$13 million** and claimed over **5.6 million meals** in approximately 16 months. Those are alleged reimbursement claims and meal counts, not adjudicated losses, amounts collected, or funds available for forfeiture.

**D. Transaction evidence alleged (PDF pp.10–14, ¶¶36–37 and 46–51):** Prosecutors allege a **$796,485.60** transfer into Fresh Point LLC from Addis Foundation in June 2021 and a **$400,000** Fresh Point transfer to a title company. They describe Marcon LLC buying three contiguous parcels on July 16, 2021 for **$516,058.50 including closing expenses**:

**Source-level discrepancy:** The four component amounts in the extracted text total $516,058.00, $0.50 less than the complaint's stated $516,058.50. Preserve both figures and obtain PDF page-image/closing-statement confirmation; do not insert a balancing component.

No double count: the **$400,000 is within the $516,058.50**, and the $516,058.50 is historic purchase consideration, not forfeiture proceeds collected. DOJ's approximate **$519,000** October 2026 group property valuation is a distinct measurement, not interchangeable with the 2021 closing price. The DOJ press release does not provide verified net realization.

**E. Potential claimant vs defendant person (PDF p.16, ¶61):** Marcon LLC is identified as known **potential claimant** in this **in-rem** civil proceeding. The case names real property as defendants; the complaint does not criminally convict Marcon, its owners, other vendors or PIN.

**F. Verification (PDF p.19):** FBI Special Agent Travis Wilmer verifies the complaint under penalty of perjury, including assertions based on information and belief. Verification does not convert each asserted underlying event into a judicial finding.
