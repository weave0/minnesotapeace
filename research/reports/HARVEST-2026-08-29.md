## 9. Publication rules preserved

- A sentence is an adjudicated court outcome; a press release is evidence of that outcome but the judgment remains the best source for exact financial obligations.
- A guilty plea resolves guilt as to the pleaded offense; it does not make every amount in every earlier charging allegation a `proven_loss` figure.
- A restitution order is not a recovered-cash total.
- A program-integrity case is not necessarily a criminal case.
- A flagged application is not necessarily completed fraud.
- No political or institutional actor is assigned criminal intent without actor-specific primary evidence.
