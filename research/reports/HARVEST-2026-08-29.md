## 5. Medical Assistance Fraud law — controlling-source gap closed

### Enacted statute

Primary: **2026 Minn. Laws ch. 127, art. 7**, creating Minn. Stat. § 609.467.  
Source record: `research/sources/src-mn-laws-2026-ch127-art7-medical-assistance-fraud.json`.

Penalty ceilings:

- loss ≤ $100,000 → up to 10 years / $20,000 fine;
- loss > $100,000 through $1M → up to 20 years / $100,000 fine;
- loss > $1M → up to 30 years / $1M fine.

The statute also contains a restitution provision for certain similar related conduct, with notice and prosecution burden requirements.

**Effective Aug. 1, 2026 for crimes committed on or after that date.** Do not project the new offense or penalties backward onto earlier conduct.

### 2026 Sentencing Guidelines

Primary: Minnesota Sentencing Guidelines Commission.  
Source record: `research/sources/src-mn-msgc-2026-medical-assistance-fraud-rankings.json`.

Confirmed offense rankings:

- ≤ $100k → Severity Level 2;
- $100,001–$1M → Severity Level 6;
- > $1M → Severity Level 8.

Severity level is not itself an individual sentence; criminal history, grid rules, departures and other sentencing law still matter.

The Aug. 3 Attorney General release is retained as an agency explanation:

- `research/sources/src-mn-ag-2026-08-03-medicaid-fraud-sentencing-law.json`

## 6. Emerging family — higher-education enrollment / student-aid identity fraud

Opened at:

- `research/harvest/higher-education/README.md`

### Official prevention baseline

**2026 Minn. Laws ch. 116** appropriates **$3M in FY2027** to Minnesota State for automated identity-verification systems to combat enrollment fraud. It is a one-time appropriation available through **June 30, 2029**.

Source record:

- `research/sources/src-mn-laws-2026-ch116-enrollment-fraud-id-verification.json`

The $3M is `program_spend` for prevention/control — **not fraud loss**.

### Current reporting lead

KSTP reported on 2026-08-24 that Normandale administrators said roughly **29,000 fraudulent or potentially fraudulent applications** had been processed over several years; roughly **31%** of current applications were being flagged for review; Minnesota State flagged **2,696 fraudulent or potentially fraudulent financial-aid applications** in 2025-26; and one identity-theft victim's information was reportedly used for an approximately **$9,000 federal student loan**.

Source record:

- `research/sources/src-kstp-2026-08-24-ghost-student-fraud.json`

Required interpretation:

- application flagged ≠ fraud proven;
- application count ≠ perpetrator count;
- attempted enrollment ≠ aid disbursed;
- aid disbursed ≠ final loss;
- Normandale percentage ≠ statewide fraud rate;
- one victim's $9k loan ≠ statewide loss estimate.

Next primary sources: Minnesota State/Normandale system data defining flags and dispositions, identity-verification procurement/implementation records, U.S. Department of Education/FSA records, and any OLA/ED-OIG work or criminal cases.

## 9. Publication rules preserved

- Government sentencing release can establish the sentence event; the judgment remains preferred for exact financial obligations.
- Guilty plea resolves guilt as to the pleaded offense, not every dollar characterization in an earlier charging document.
- Restitution ordered ≠ restitution collected.
- Identified for recovery ≠ recovered cash.
- Payment withhold/disenrollment/sanction ≠ criminal conviction.
- Flagged application ≠ completed fraud.
- Statutory maximum ≠ presumptive sentence.
- Effective date matters.
- No political or institutional actor receives criminal intent by inference.
