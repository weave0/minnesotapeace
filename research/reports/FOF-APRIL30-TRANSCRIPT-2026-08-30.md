# Feeding Our Future — April 30, 2021 hearing transcript follow-up

**As of:** 30 August 2026

This pass closes the last material factual ambiguity in the Ramsey County payment-resumption sequence without claiming more than the record supports.

## What the April 30 transcript establishes

The public mirror of the official Ramsey County District Court transcript for the April 30, 2021 hearing in **62-CV-20-5492** records Assistant Attorney General Kristine Nogosek stating on behalf of MDE that the department was no longer continuing the stop-pay on Feeding Our Future's reimbursement claims and that the stop-pay had been removed.

The same transcript narrows an important timing distinction. MDE counsel told the court that the claims were expected to show in CLiCS after 5:00 p.m. that day as scheduled to be paid the following Thursday or Friday. Accordingly:

- **April 30 is well-supported as the administrative stop-pay-removal / return-to-normal-processing date.**
- It should **not** automatically be described as the date every reimbursement dollar cleared or was received.
- The later June 24 order establishes that the relevant reimbursement claims had been paid by then.

This complements, rather than replaces, the earlier sources. FOF's April 30 reply quoted MDE's written opposition saying the stop-pay was removed pursuant to the court's April 21 guidance. The April 30 transcript now independently records MDE's changed position on the record. OLA and the Minnesota Judicial Branch separately confirm that no court order compelled payment resumption.

## What remains open

The standalone full text of MDE's April 30 written opposition brief is still uncaptured. It remains useful for exact written phrasing and any legal arguments not aired orally, but it is no longer a blocker to the factual chronology.

The exact date on which every reimbursement payment cleared or was received also remains distinct from the stop-pay-removal date. The transcript provides an expected processing/payment schedule but not bank-settlement evidence.

The transcript also strengthens, but does not resolve, the **149 vs. 143** site-application count issue. Judge Guthmann referred on April 30 to an MDE letter containing 149 entries marked "site ID denied"; the June 24 order later described 143 of 144 site-ID applications as denied. The reason for that difference remains unproven.

## Publication consequence

Public language should say that **MDE voluntarily removed the stop-pay on April 30, 2021 after the April 21 hearing, without a court order requiring payment resumption**. If discussing when money actually moved, use more precise language and do not equate stop-pay removal with same-day receipt of all funds.
