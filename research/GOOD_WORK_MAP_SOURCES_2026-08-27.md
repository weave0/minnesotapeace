# Good Work Map — source ledger

Verified 2026-08-27 (America/Chicago). This file is research-side only and must not be copied into the public deployment artifact.

## Editorial rule

The public `/good/` map separates:

1. **Reported / ongoing work** — a city, agency, or program source says the activity occurred or is ongoing.
2. **Funding approved** — a grantmaker or authoritative source says funding was approved for a named project. This is **not** treated as proof that the project was completed or reached a particular number of people.

Public agencies are not assigned partisan labels. Republican / conservative labels remain confined to organizations that self-identify or are authoritatively identified as such. Formal DFL electoral organizations remain outside this particular editorial series by disclosed scope.

## Local execution / ongoing-program sources

### Bemidji — Casting with Cops
Primary city sources:
- https://www.ci.bemidji.mn.us/index.asp?SEC=%7BE649C73D-2FB0-460A-8D74-BAA2716D5E7B%7D
  - Current City of Bemidji Community Outreach page lists Casting with Cops among police outreach programs.
- https://www.ci.bemidji.mn.us/uploads/dm/73247/State_of_the_City_2024
  - City reporting says the 2024 partnership between Police, Parks & Recreation, and local sponsors gave **100 rod/reel combinations** to community youth and generated **more than $9,000** in cash/in-kind support.
- https://www.ci.bemidji.mn.us/vertical/sites/%7BDC40720D-A823-4643-91CD-6456402D11F6%7D/uploads/10-21-2024_CC_Packet.pdf
  - Council item documents $7,450 cash + $1,800 in-kind = $9,250 and describes youth fishing with Bemidji Police, Beltrami County, and DNR officers.
- MCPF 2025 recipient list independently shows Casting with Cops funded again.

Public claim level: **reported work + continuing/funded again**.

### Moorhead — Youth and Family Services / Explorers / community events
- https://www.moorheadmn.gov/emergency-services/police/youth-and-family-services/
  - Youth Services Coordinator mentors students; collaborates with police, probation, social services, and shelter care; oversees community-service referrals; Police Explorer post established in 2004 and generally maintains 12–18 members.
- https://www.moorheadmn.gov/emergency-services/police/community-events-and-programs/
  - City frames community events as relationship-building; includes Safe City Nights, Night to Unite, and a police volunteer program.

Public claim level: **ongoing city programs**.

### Duluth — Police Explorers
- https://duluthmn.gov/police/citizen-opportunities/police-explorers/
  - Program meets nearly every Sunday evening September–April.
  - Goals include practical law-enforcement experience, personal development, supportive leadership, and youth leadership opportunities.
  - Explorers compete in the statewide Rochester conference at season end.

Public claim level: **ongoing youth program**.

### St. Cloud — Southside Hoops
- https://ci.stcloud.mn.us/1804/Southside-Hoops
  - Free 3-on-3 league at Haws Park.
  - St. Cloud Police officers organize and referee basketball.
  - Mission is to provide a safe youth environment and foster youth/officer relationships naturally through the game.
  - City page says approximately **300 participants ages 8–18**.
- https://www.ci.stcloud.mn.us/1099/Community-Programs-Events
  - Also documents broader COP House programs including fishing, soccer, hockey, lifting and Southside Hoops.

Public claim level: **ongoing youth program**.

### Rochester — Safe City Nights
- https://www.rochestermn.gov/news/posts/rpd-announces-return-of-safe-city-nights/
  - City announced Safe City Nights returning in 2026.
- https://www.rochestermn.gov/home/showpublisheddocument/43381/638766856288670000
  - Official 2024 summary reports an estimated **5,500 attendees** and about 30 public-safety/city/county partners.
- https://www.rochestermn.gov/law-emergency-services/police/community-engagement/
  - Current community-engagement page says trust and relationships are central to collaborative safety work.

Public claim level: **ongoing city program; 2024 reported attendance**.
