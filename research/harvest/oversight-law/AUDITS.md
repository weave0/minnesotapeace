# AUDITS.md — OLA / HHS OIG / USDA OIG / GAO / CMS PERM

**Compiled:** 26 August 2026.  
**Rule:** Audit findings support **B** (oversight failure) or **C** (negligent/inadequate administration) unless the auditor documents a specific stolen-dollar finding against a named private actor (**A**). Audits almost never establish **D** (official criminality).

Feeding Our Future is **in** this file (OLA 2024) because it is the baseline special review; the task asked for *additional* audits **beyond** FOF — those are marked **BEYOND-FOF**.

## 4. GAO (national improper payments — **BEYOND-FOF**, not MN-specific)

### 4.1 GAO-26-108694 — FY 2025 improper payments

- Product: https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-26-108694
- PDF: https://www.gao.gov/assets/gao-26-108694.pdf
- National total ~$186 billion estimated improper payments FY 2025; **Medicaid ~$37 billion**. HHS attributed Medicaid increase partly to eligibility redeterminations and provider screening as COVID flexibilities ended.
- **Improper payment ≠ fraud.** About 82% of the government-wide estimate was overpayments (still includes documentation errors).

### 4.2 GAO-25-107770 — Medicaid managed-care PERM gap

- https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-25-107770
- CMS’s managed-care PERM component checks state capitation to plans, **not** plan payments to providers. A 0% MC PERM rate (Minnesota RY 2025) does not measure waiver/FFS provider fraud.

## 5. CMS PERM and Minnesota CAP — **BEYOND-FOF** (Medicaid improper payments / program integrity)

### 5.1 PERM rules

- CAP process: https://www.cms.gov/data-research/monitoring-programs/improper-payment-measurement-programs/payment-error-rate-measurement-perm/corrective-action-plan-cap-process

### 5.2 Minnesota RY 2025 PERM results (CMS letter 20 January 2026)

| Component | Sample | Improper-payment rate | 95% CI |
|---|---|---|---|
| Overall | 900 | **2.16%** | 0.76–3.56% |
| FFS | 605 | 1.32% | 0.44–2.20% |
| Managed care | 69 | 0.00% | 0.00–0.00% |
| Eligibility | 226 | 1.52% | 0.18–2.87% |

- PDF (MN host of CMS letter): https://mn.gov/dhs/assets/2025-medicaid-error-notification_tcm1053-720555.pdf
- Local: `pdfs/mn-perm-ry2025-notification.pdf`
- Review window: payments **1 July 2023 – 30 June 2024**.
- CMS: improper payments include missing documentation; “technically improper” payments may not be recovered. COVID PHE flexibilities still affect comparability.
- **Do not equate 2.16% with a fraud rate, and do not use it to validate or refute $9B.** Different universe, method, and error definition.
