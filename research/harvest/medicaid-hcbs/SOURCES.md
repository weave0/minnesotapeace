### Quoted holdings from DOJ-HSS-1 (2025-09-18)

- "Eight defendants have been federally charged with wire fraud" — first wave.
- HSS "dates back to July 2022" in this release (see GAPS.md: later DOJ and DHS say July 2020).
- Predicted cost about $2.6 million annually; paid out more than $21 million (2021), $42 million (2022), $74 million (2023), $104 million (2024), $61 million first six months of 2025. Tags: program_spend / amount_paid. Class: CHARGED_ALLEGED as stated in charging-document narrative; treat as government allegation of program cost, not proven fraud.
- Brilliant Minds: claims totaling about $2.3 million (amount_billed); each of four defendants "personally pocketed between about $300,000 and $400,000."
- Faladcare: claimed "over $2.2 million" (amount_billed).
- Leo Human Services: "received about $2.7 million" (amount_paid).
- Liberty Plus: "received more than $1.2 million" (amount_paid).
- Dockets: U.S. v. Moktar Aden et al., 25-cr-349 (MJD/JFD); U.S. v. Christopher Falade et al., 25-cr-351 (JMB/DJF); U.S. v. Asad Ahmed Adow, 25-cr-354 (ADM); U.S. v. Anwar Ahmed Adow, 25-cr-353 (PAM).
- Standard caveat: "An indictment is merely an allegation..."

### Quoted holdings from DOJ-EIDBI-1 (2025-09-24)

- Asha Farhan Hassan, 28, charged by federal information with wire fraud for a $14 million autism fraud scheme (amount_paid / alleged_loss — PR says Smart Therapy "obtaining more than $14 million in EIDBI reimbursement funds from Minnesota DHS and UCare").
- Separate Feeding Our Future participation: claimed entitlement to approximately $465,000 in Federal Child Nutrition Program funds (amount_billed / alleged_loss).
- Acting U.S. Attorney Joseph H. Thompson: "Today's charges mark the first in the ongoing investigation into fraud in the EIDBI Autism Program."
- Thompson also said schemes "form a web that has stolen billions of dollars in taxpayer money." Tag: fraud_estimate. Not a proven_loss. No methodology in this PR.

### Quoted holdings from DOJ-DEC18 (2025-12-18)

- Six additional defendants charged in Autism and HSS schemes; Asha Farhan Hassan pleaded guilty to one count of wire fraud (ADJUDICATED plea; sentencing later).
- Abdinajib Hassan Yussuf, 27, Star Autism Center LLC president/CEO, charged by information; Star Autism "obtaining more than $6 million in EIDBI reimbursement funds" (amount_paid / alleged_loss).
- HSS: Anthony Waddell Jefferson, 37, and Lester Brown, 53 (Philadelphia) — "submitted about $3.5 million in HSS claims" (amount_billed).
- HSS: Hassan Ahmed Hussein, 28, and Ahmed Abdirashid Mohamed, 27 (Pristine Health LLC) — claimed "about $750,000" (amount_billed).
- HSS: Kaamil Omar Sallah, 26 (SafeLodgings, Inc.) — claimed approximately $1.4 million, received nearly $1.3 million; fled to Amsterdam about 2025-11-26 (amount_billed / amount_paid).
- ICS: court unsealed search warrant for Ultimate Home Health Services LLC. Program paid about $4.6 million (2021), more than $170 million (2024), more than $400 million since 2021 (program_spend / amount_paid). Ultimate submitted claims for over $1.1 million ICS funds June 2024-August 2025 (amount_billed).

### Quoted holdings from DOJ-OPA-0521 / DOJ-SUMM (2026-05-21 / updated 2026-07-29)

- 15 defendants; "over $90 million in intended loss" (intended_loss — includes child-care schemes outside HCBS scope).
- EIDBI: Shamso Ahmed Hassan, 55, and Hanaan Mursal Yusuf, 25 — $46.6 million scheme, of which approximately $21.2 million was paid (amount_billed / amount_paid). "Largest Medicaid autism fraud case ever charged by the Department."
- EIDBI claims "skyrocketed from over $600,000 in 2018 to over $400 million by 2025" (program_spend).
- ICS first criminal prosecution: Ahmed Kadar / Ahmed Othman Kadar, 22 — approximately $1.4 million billed and paid (amount_billed / amount_paid).
- IHS first criminal prosecution: Charles Wayne Healey, 61, and Katherin Suzan Larsen-Guthmiller, 66 — received $22.7 million (OPA said "over $22 million"; case summary: $22.7 million) (amount_paid / alleged_loss). IHS program "grew to cost more than $700 million in 2025" (program_spend).
- HSS this wave: eight defendants, approximately $15.7 million (alleged_loss as stated by OPA; mix of billed/paid in case summaries).
- HSS shuttered October 31, 2025. 2021 paid "more than $26 million" in this OPA release vs "more than $21 million" in Sept/Dec 2025 USAO releases (see GAPS).
