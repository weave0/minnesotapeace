# GAPS.md — What this pass did not obtain, and known inconsistencies
Compiled 2026-08-26.

## Biggest gaps (priority order)

1. **Federal charging instruments (PACER).** USAO press releases give docket numbers for the Sept. 2025 HSS wave (25-cr-349, 351, 353, 354) but informations/indictments, plea agreements, and any restitution/forfeiture orders were not pulled. Without those: no proven_loss, no stipulated-loss, no restitution_ordered, no forfeiture_ordered columns.

2. **CMS’s own Dec. 5, 2025 and Jan. 6, 2026 letters** are not posted on cms.gov. Content is known only via MN DHS CAP letters, the March 19 Brandt approval letter, and secondary quotes. Need the PDFs from CMS, FOIA, or the Minnesota v. HHS/CMS docket.

3. **$9 billion fraud_estimate is not in any written DOJ press release.** It is a Dec. 18, 2025 press-conference Q&A soundbite (Joseph H. Thompson: 14 programs billed $18B since 2018; "half or more" possibly fraudulent). No official transcript, slide, or methodology document was found. The $18B denominator is journalism-reported, not on justice.gov.

4. **Yussuf / Star Autism guilty plea** is widely reported (Star Tribune, Pioneer Press, KSTP, KARE 11) as March 2, 2026, with sentencing deferred. No USAO-MN press release confirming the plea. Need PACER minute entry / plea agreement.

5. **Optum Phase 1 report** (redacted) is circulating as a KSTP-hosted PDF (https://kstp.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/MN-PI-Phase-1-Summary-Report-Final-Redacted.pdf). Snippets claim ~$52.3M identified recoveries and >$1.7B "opportunities" on FFS claims in the 14 categories (MCO out of scope). Full official hosting on mn.gov was not confirmed this pass. Those figures must not enter CLAIMS as verified until the document is captured and quoted.

6. **No ARMHS, ACT, IRTS, NEMT, Recovery Peer, Recuperative Care, Night Supervision, Adult Day, or CFSS federal indictment** was found in USAO-MN 2025-2026 PRs, except ARMHS via state MFCU (Elmi/Reva, Aug. 2026) and companion/IHS/PCA via Omarxeyd (Jan. 2026). High-risk designation is not the same as charged fraud in each category.

7. **House Oversight Committee report and transcribed interviews** (Walz/Ellison March 2026 hearing) were not captured as primary PDFs. DHS fact-check page quotes and disputes them (LEGISLATIVE_FINDING_OR_ALLEGATION vs AGENCY_POSITION). Needed to map B/C without collapsing into D.

8. **Sentencing / restitution outcomes** for Asha Hassan and any first-wave HSS defendants: not found.

9. **MFCU annual statistical reports** (HHS-OIG MFCU reports) for FFY 2024-2026 were not pulled; would independently check "340 convictions / $80-90M judgments" claims in AG PRs.

10. **Managed-care (MCO) share of the 14 categories.** Optum Phase 1 reportedly excluded MCO encounters. USAO amounts mix DHS and UCare. No official split of HSS/EIDBI/ICS/IHS spend FFS vs MCO.
