## 1. The 14 high-risk Medicaid service categories (August 2026)

Quoted from DHS Program Integrity, "High-risk provider types":

1. Adult Companion Services
2. Adult Day Services
3. Adult Rehabilitative Mental Health Services
4. Assertive Community Treatment
5. Community First Services and Supports
6. Early Intensive Developmental and Behavioral Intervention
7. Housing Stabilization Services
8. Individualized Home Supports
9. Integrated Community Supports
10. Intensive Residential Treatment Services
11. Night Supervision Services
12. Nonemergency Medical Transportation Services
13. Recovery Peer Support
14. Recuperative Care

Stated basis (AGENCY_POSITION, same page):
> "DHS has identified 14 Medicaid service provider types as 'high-risk' based on programmatic vulnerabilities, evidence of fraudulent activity, or data analytics that revealed potentially suspicious patterns, claim anomalies or outliers. This designation requires providers to undergo additional screening measures both at the time of enrollment and during revalidation."

Same basis language in DHS-CAP-1231 (Dec. 31, 2025): "tips, referrals, investigative findings, data analysis, and an assessment of program vulnerabilities."

Timeline of designation (DHS-PI "Timeline of actions taken"):
- May 2025: "First additional high-risk designations" — EIDBI and HSS labeled high-risk.
- October 2025: "Second additional high-risk designations" — nine additional Medicaid services rated high risk. (That plus the May pair and any already-high-risk types yields the 14.)

14 vs 13: HSS benefit terminated October 31, 2025 (CMS-approved SPA). Operational revalidation/moratorium documents often say 13 remaining high-risk services. HSS remains on the 14-item list because of historical billing and residual integrity work.

Actor bucket: this list is a B oversight response. It is not a finding that every claim in these categories is fraudulent, and it is not D.

Duplicate official list: January 13, 2026 MHCP Provider News PDF, https://mn.gov/dhs/assets/2026-01-13-mhcp-provider-news_tcm1053-721713.pdf (pre-payment review via Optum).

## 7. Related 2025-2026 statutory / executive overlay (B/C, not D)

- Governor Executive Order 25-10 (Sept. 17, 2025) — cited throughout CAP and PI page.
- 2025 session: EIDBI licensure phase-in; anti-kickback for public programs; expanded withhold authority (DHS-FACT).
- 2026 MAP Act (Medical Assistance Protection Act): MFCU +18 staff (11 investigators, 3 attorneys, 4 support; 32 to 50), higher penalties, longer SOL, expanded subpoena, recovery tools (AG-ARMHS-0817; DHS-PI-JUL).
- 2026 HHS omnibus (DHS-PI-JUL): ARMHS licensing; ICS 15-minute units + acuity limits; EVV expansion to most high-risk and additional HCBS; HSS statutory repeal.

AG-HSS-1 (Sept. 18, 2025) records that the 2025 Legislature denied Ellison's request for 9 additional MFCU positions despite 75% federal match — a C-type resource fact, not a crime.

EIDBI site-visit stats (DHS-FACT): 444 onsite visits; 73 new EIDBI investigations; 25 EIDBI law-enforcement referrals; 35 EIDBI administrative sanctions (since Oct. 2024 scrutiny increase). CAP-0130: 324 announced EIDBI visits Oct. 2024-June 2025. Record both; they are different windows/definitions.
