# Feeding Our Future — Primary Source List

Corpus compiled 2026-08-26 (America/Chicago). Preference: DOJ/USAO-MN, IRS-CI (USAO republications), court opinions/plea agreements, Minnesota OLA PDFs, Minnesota Judicial Branch. Journalism is recorded only as a lead.

USAO-MN press-release URLs often return Akamai interstitial (403/empty). Where that happened, the IRS-CI republication of the same USAO text is treated as the readable primary. Do not treat `$9 billion` as a figure from these sources; it does not appear in them.

Evidence-class key used in “what it supports”: ADJUDICATED | CHARGED_ALLEGED | CIVIL_ALLEGED_OR_RESOLVED | AUDIT_FINDING | AGENCY_POSITION | SWORN_TESTIMONY | LEGISLATIVE_FINDING_OR_ALLEGATION | SECONDARY_VERIFIED | LEAD_UNVERIFIED | DERIVED_INFERENCE.
