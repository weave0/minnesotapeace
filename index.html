// 01 — Why

Awareness and acceptance
are the path to repair.

This is MN Peace: a public frame for community healing so Minnesota can look at the record with eyes open. The primary work is peace — not a thriller dashboard.

What the domain holds now is a public record, and a way to stand in front of it without turning neighbors into a spectacle.

Awareness is not accusation of a community. A shared address is not a conspiracy. Facing documents is how repair starts. Hope without naivete looks like that.

How we heal

01

Hold the facts at the grain they were written. Leave room for people who never appear in a caption. Do not ask a whole community to carry a case number. The honest layer is the record. The human layer is Minnesota.

// 02 — Reading

A charge is not a conviction.

Read slowly. The labels are doing work a headline will not do.

  • Charged means a prosecutor wrote it in an instrument. It is not a conviction.
  • Billed is not paid. Paid is not alleged loss. Alleged loss is not proven.
  • When two figures describe the same money, do not add them.
  • A guilty plea in a docket minute is not a sentence.

How to read

02

Charged is a filing. Convicted is a judgment. This site does not skip that distance.

// 04 — Limits

We will not make a spectacle
out of a number.

A healing site can still be exact. These are promises about what will not appear here.

01

We will not print a statewide fraud total

These cases share money, sponsors, and recitals. Adding them into one homepage figure would count the same dollars more than once. There is no TOTAL FRAUD on this page.

02

A speech is not a judgment

Circulating estimates stay estimates. If a number is not in a charging document or a court judgment, it does not headline this site.

03

Missing the theft is not committing the theft

This record follows private actors charged with fraud against public programs. It does not accuse a named official of joining that crime.

04

No community is on trial

A charging document names defendants. It does not indict a language, a faith, a neighborhood, or a diaspora. A shared address is not a conspiracy.

// 05 — Community

Minnesota is more
than a case caption.

Saint Paul, Minneapolis, and the towns between them are where people live, worship, work, and raise kids — including people who never appear in these files.

Repair is a civic practice: keep the record honest, keep the frame humane, and leave room for a future that is not defined by the worst documents in it. Inclusive means the door stays open while the facts stay labeled.
