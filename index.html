// 01 — Why

Awareness and acceptance
are the path to repair.

This is MN Peace: a public frame for community healing so Minnesota can look at the record with eyes open. The primary work is peace — not a thriller dashboard.

The mediation practice that used to live on this domain is closed. What the domain holds now is a public record, and a way to stand in front of it without turning neighbors into a spectacle.

Awareness is not accusation of a community. Shared address is not conspiracy. Facing documents is how repair starts. Hope without naivete looks like that.

How we heal

01

Hold the facts at the grain they were written. Leave room for people who never appear in a caption. Do not ask a whole community to carry a case number. The honest layer is the record. The human layer is Minnesota.

// 02 — Reading

The atomic unit
is a claim.

A claim is a sentence with a source and a status. It is not an article, and it is not a verdict. Read slowly. The labels are doing work.

  • CHARGED_ALLEGED is what a charging instrument says. It is not a conviction.
  • Every displayed dollar keeps a metric_type — billed is not paid; paid is not alleged loss; alleged loss is not proven loss.
  • Figures that share an overlap group are restatements or nested amounts. Do not sum them.
  • Do not upgrade charged to sentenced from journalism. A guilty-plea docket minute is not a judgment.

// 04 — Limits

Limits are
part of the peace.

A healing site can still be exact. These refusals are editorial, not a vibe.

Editorial, not a vibe

These refusals travel with the record. They are how this site stays a peace frame instead of a spectacle.

01

No homepage fraud total

We will not publish a TOTAL FRAUD number. Overlapping dollars stay in their overlap groups. There is no corpus-wide loss on this page.

02

No press-conference arithmetic as proven loss

Circulating estimates are not treated as adjudicated loss. If a figure is not in the claim files with a metric_type, it does not headline this site.

03

Oversight failure is not official crime

Actor classes B and C are not collapsed into D or E. v1 does not claim that a named official knowingly committed the theft.

04

No ethnic or communal guilt

A charging instrument names defendants. It does not indict a language, a faith, a neighborhood, or a diaspora. Shared address is not conspiracy.

// 05 — Community

Minnesota is more
than a case caption.

Saint Paul, Minneapolis, and the towns between them are where people live, worship, work, and raise kids — including people who never appear in these files.

Repair is a civic practice: keep the record honest, keep the frame humane, and leave room for a future that is not defined by the worst documents in it. Inclusive means the door stays open while the facts stay labeled.
