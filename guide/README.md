# Public Field Guide

This directory is part of the visitor-facing publication layer.

Purpose: make Minnesota's public-program fraud story legible to readers who do not already know Minnesota institutions, U.S. federalism, federal criminal procedure, or public-program reimbursement terminology.

Editorial rules:
- distinguish government agencies from private sponsors/providers;
- distinguish criminal allegations from audit/oversight findings;
- distinguish charging documents from convictions and sentences;
- keep dollar labels attached to the measurement they describe;
- prefer official and primary sources;
- do not turn organization or identity lists into guilt-by-association graphics.
